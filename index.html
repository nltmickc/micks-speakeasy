The Classics

  • Negroni

    Equal measures of gin, Campari and sweet vermouth, stirred over ice and finished with orange peel.

  • Old Fashioned

    Bourbon coaxed open with demerara and aromatic bitters, garnished with Maraschino cherries.

  • Boulevardier

    The Negroni's whiskey cousin — bourbon, Campari and sweet vermouth, stirred long and served cold.

  • Sidecar

    Cognac shaken with orange liqueur and fresh lemon, poured into a sugar-rimmed glass.

  • Perfect Manhattan

    Bourbon balanced between sweet and dry vermouth, finished with aromatic bitters.

  • Paper Plane

    Bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino and fresh lemon in equal measure, shaken bright and balanced.

  • Manhattan

    Rye whiskey and sweet vermouth, kissed with bitters and a single brandied cherry.

  • Bramble

    London dry gin and fresh lemon over crushed ice, laced with a slow bleed of blackberry liqueur.

  • Sazerac

    Rye, Cognac and bitters, sweetened with cane sugar and Maraschino cherries.

  • Classic Martini

    Gin and dry vermouth, stirred cold and clear, finished with an olive or a twist of lemon.

  • Metropolitan

    Brandy stirred with sweet vermouth, sugar and bitters — a Manhattan gone continental.

  • Penicillin

    Blended Scotch, honey-ginger and fresh lemon, floated with a whisper of smoky Islay malt.
