Recipes

The Classics

Negroni

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth

Stir all ingredients over ice, strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice, and garnish with an orange peel.

Old Fashioned

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 1 sugar cube (or ¼ oz simple syrup)
  • 2–3 dashes aromatic bitters

Muddle the sugar with bitters and a splash of water, add bourbon and ice, and stir until chilled. Garnish with an orange peel and a Maraschino cherry.

Boulevardier

  • 1 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth

Stir over ice and strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice. Garnish with an orange twist.

Sidecar

  • 2 oz cognac
  • ¾ oz orange liqueur
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Shake hard with ice and strain into a sugar-rimmed coupe.

Perfect Manhattan

  • 2 oz rye or bourbon
  • ½ oz sweet vermouth
  • ½ oz dry vermouth
  • 2 dashes aromatic bitters

Stir over ice and strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a brandied cherry.

Paper Plane

  • ¾ oz bourbon
  • ¾ oz Aperol
  • ¾ oz Amaro Nonino
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice

Shake hard with ice and strain into a chilled coupe.

Manhattan

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes aromatic bitters

Stir over ice and strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a brandied cherry.

Bramble

  • 2 oz London dry gin
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ½ oz simple syrup
  • ½ oz blackberry liqueur (crème de mûre)

Build the gin, lemon and syrup over crushed ice and stir. Drizzle the blackberry liqueur over the top so it bleeds through. Garnish with a blackberry and lemon slice.

Sazerac

  • 2 oz rye whiskey (or cognac)
  • 1 sugar cube
  • 3 dashes Peychaud's bitters
  • Absinthe, for rinsing

Rinse a chilled rocks glass with absinthe and discard the excess. Muddle the sugar cube with bitters, add rye and ice, and stir until chilled. Strain into the prepared glass and express a lemon peel over the top before discarding it.

Classic Martini

  • 2½ oz gin
  • ½ oz dry vermouth

Stir over ice and strain into a chilled martini glass. Garnish with an olive or a lemon twist.

Metropolitan

  • 2 oz brandy
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • ¼ oz simple syrup
  • 2 dashes aromatic bitters

Stir over ice and strain into a chilled coupe.

Penicillin

  • 2 oz blended Scotch
  • ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
  • ¾ oz honey-ginger syrup
  • ¼ oz smoky Islay single malt, for floating

Shake the Scotch, lemon and honey-ginger syrup with ice and strain over fresh ice in a rocks glass. Float the Islay malt on top. Garnish with candied ginger.
